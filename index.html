Camera for iPhone

A camera that explains what your eye already knows.

Fovea is a pro iPhone camera with manual controls, RAW, live film looks and effects, and an insight overlay that explains your settings in plain language as you shoot.

  • Free: priority modes, exposure compensation, white balance presets, the histogram, live effects, the insight overlay, HEIF and JPEG.
  • One-time unlock: full manual, manual focus with peaking and zebra, RAW and ProRAW, long exposure, bracketing, the intervalometer, every look and the shot journal. No subscription.
  • Private: no account, no analytics, no tracking. Your photos stay yours.
