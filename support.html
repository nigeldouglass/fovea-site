Get in touch

Questions, bugs or ideas? Every message is read. Email fovea@nigeldouglass.com.

To get an answer faster, include your app version (Settings › About › Version), your iPhone model and iOS version, and a screenshot if you're reporting a bug.

  • Something's brokenA crash, a photo that didn't save, or a result that looks wrong. Say what you did just before.
  • Wish it did moreTell me what's missing. Feature requests shape what comes next.
  • Need a handStuck on a shot? Describe what you're trying to do and I'll point you in the right direction.

Prefer to find it yourself? See the help topics below.

Restoring the unlock

The unlock is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account, so it follows you to every iPhone you sign in on.

  1. Open Settings (the button at the top left of the camera).
  2. Under About, tap Restore purchase.
  3. Sign in with the Apple Account you bought Fovea with, if you're asked.

If it says there's nothing to restore, check you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, then look in your purchase history in the App Store. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
